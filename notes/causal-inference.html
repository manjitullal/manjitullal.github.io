Causal Inference: Study Resources

Free online books I use to prepare for causal inference questions in data science interviews.

Causal Inference: The Mixtape

Scott Cunningham. An intuitive, econometrics-flavored introduction: potential outcomes, DAGs, matching, regression discontinuity, instrumental variables, panel data, difference-in-differences, and synthetic control. Code in R, Stata, and Python.

Causal Inference for the Brave and True

Matheus Facure. A hands-on Python handbook. Part I covers the classic toolkit (randomized experiments, regression, propensity scores, IV, diff-in-diff); Part II covers heterogeneous treatment effects and ML-based estimators such as meta-learners and double/debiased ML.

Causal Inference and Machine Learning

Mutlu Yuksel and Yigit Aydede. Moves from basic econometric and causal concepts to modern machine learning methods, aimed at economics, social, and health sciences, with R code and simulations throughout.
